# Services Page Content Generation Prompt

## Objective

Generate clear, persuasive, and website-ready service descriptions
for a local business.

## Role

Act as an expert website copywriter specializing in local
business websites and conversion-focused service pages.

## Business Information

Business Name: UrbanGlow Salon

Business Type: Unisex Salon

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka

## Target Audience

- Young professionals
- Students
- Families
- Local residents

## Services

- Haircuts
- Hair Styling
- Hair Coloring
- Facial
- Manicure & Pedicure
- Bridal Packages

## Requirements

For each service, generate:

1. Service name
2. Short description
3. What the service includes
4. Key customer benefit
5. Suggested call-to-action

## Content Guidelines

The content must:

- Be specific to the business
- Focus on customer benefits
- Be easy to understand
- Be concise and professional
- Sound natural and human
- Be suitable for a real website
- Avoid generic AI language
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Avoid excessive marketing language

## Tone

Use a:

- Friendly
- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Confident

tone.

## Local Context

The business is located in Bangalore, Karnataka.

Use the location naturally where relevant.

Do not repeat location keywords unnecessarily.

## Output Format

Create a separate section for each service.

Use the following structure:

### Service Name

**Description:**  
Short website-ready description.

**What's Included:**  
List the main inclusions.

**Customer Benefit:**  
Explain the value to the customer.

**CTA:**  
Provide a short action-oriented call-to-action.

## Final Requirement

The generated content should be ready to publish on
a professional local business website.
