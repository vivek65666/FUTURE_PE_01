# CTA Generation Prompt

## Objective

Generate persuasive and conversion-focused call-to-action
sections for a local business website.

## Role

Act as a conversion-focused website copywriter specializing
in local businesses.

## Business Information

Business Name: UrbanGlow Salon

Business Type: Unisex Salon

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka

## Target Audience

- Young professionals
- Students
- Families
- Local residents

## CTA Requirements

Generate multiple CTA variations for:

1. Booking an appointment
2. Contacting the salon
3. Making an enquiry
4. Visiting the salon

## CTA Structure

Each CTA should contain:

- Short headline
- Supporting sentence
- Action button text

## Content Requirements

The CTAs must:

- Be clear
- Be concise
- Be action-oriented
- Communicate customer value
- Build trust
- Encourage the visitor to take the next step
- Be suitable for a real salon website

Avoid:

- Generic phrases
- Excessive urgency
- False claims
- Overly aggressive sales language

## Tone

Use a:

- Friendly
- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Confident

tone.

## Local Context

The salon is located in Bangalore, Karnataka.

Use local context naturally where appropriate.
