# Tone Adaptation Prompt

## Objective

Adapt website copy for different types of local businesses
while maintaining clarity, relevance, and conversion focus.

## Role

Act as an expert copywriter who understands how tone should
change according to business type and customer expectations.

## Supported Business Types

- Salon
- Cafe
- Clinic
- Coaching Institute
- Agency

## Tone Rules

### Friendly

Use for:

- Salons
- Cafes

Characteristics:

- Warm
- Approachable
- Conversational
- Welcoming

### Professional

Use for:

- Clinics
- Agencies

Characteristics:

- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Informative
- Professional

### Confident but Simple

Use for:

- Coaching institutes
- Service businesses

Characteristics:

- Direct
- Encouraging
- Clear
- Results-focused without making unsupported claims

## Requirements

Given a business type, target audience, location,
services, and existing website copy:

1. Identify the appropriate tone.
2. Rewrite the copy using that tone.
3. Keep the original meaning.
4. Make the language business-specific.
5. Avoid generic AI language.
6. Keep the content suitable for a real website.

Existing Copy: [Insert copy]

## Output

Provide:

1. Selected tone
2. Reason for selecting the tone
3. Adapted website copy
4. Short explanation of improvements
