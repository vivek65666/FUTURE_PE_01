# Homepage Copy Generation Prompt

## Objective

Generate clear, persuasive, and conversion-focused homepage
copy for a local business website.

## Role

Act as an expert website copywriter and prompt engineer
specializing in local business websites.

## Business Information

Business Name: UrbanGlow Salon

Business Type: Unisex Salon

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka

## Target Audience

- Young professionals
- Students
- Families
- Local residents

## Services

- Haircuts
- Hair Styling
- Hair Coloring
- Facial
- Manicure & Pedicure
- Bridal Packages

## Homepage Requirements

Generate the following sections:

1. Hero headline
2. Supporting subheadline
3. Primary call-to-action
4. Secondary call-to-action
5. Short business introduction
6. Key customer benefits
7. Why choose us section

## Content Requirements

The content must:

- Clearly communicate the business value proposition
- Focus on customer benefits
- Be specific to the salon
- Be easy to understand
- Sound natural and human
- Be suitable for a professional website
- Encourage visitors to take action
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Avoid generic AI-generated phrases

## Tone

Use a:

- Friendly
- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Persuasive

tone.

## Local Context

Naturally incorporate the Bangalore location where appropriate.

Do not overuse location keywords.

## SEO Considerations

Naturally consider relevant terms such as:

- salon in Bangalore
- hair salon
- beauty services
- hair styling
- bridal salon

Do not keyword-stuff.

## Output Format

Return the final content using clear headings and
website-ready sections.

The content should be ready to publish on a real
local business website.
