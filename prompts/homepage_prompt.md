# Salon Nayana – Homepage Copy Generation Prompt

## Objective

Generate clear, persuasive, and conversion-focused homepage copy
for a local hair and beauty salon website.

## Role

Act as an expert website copywriter and prompt engineer
specializing in local business websites.

Create website content that is natural, customer-focused,
business-specific, and ready to publish.

---

## Business Information

Business Name: Salon Nayana

Business Type: Hair & Beauty Salon

Location: Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

## Target Audience

- Young professionals
- Students
- Local residents
- Customers looking for professional hair and beauty services
  in Bengaluru

## Services

- Haircuts
- Hair Colour
- Curls & Texture
- Beauty Services
- Keratin
- Straightening
- Hair Botox

---

## Homepage Requirements

Generate the following sections:

1. Hero headline
2. Supporting subheadline
3. Primary call-to-action
4. Secondary call-to-action
5. Short business introduction
6. Key customer benefits
7. Why choose us section
8. Services overview
9. Final call-to-action

---

## Content Requirements

The content must:

- Clearly communicate Salon Nayana's value proposition
- Focus on customer benefits
- Be specific to the salon
- Reflect the listed services
- Be easy to understand
- Sound natural and human
- Be suitable for a professional website
- Encourage visitors to take appropriate action
- Use clear and concise language
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Avoid generic AI-generated phrases
- Avoid exaggerated or unsupported claims
- Avoid invented offers, discounts, or guarantees
- Be suitable for real-world website use

---

## Tone

Use a:

- Friendly
- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Persuasive

tone.

The writing should feel conversational and approachable
without sounding overly promotional.

---

## Value Proposition

Communicate that Salon Nayana provides hair and beauty services
designed around individual customer preferences and needs.

Focus on:

- Clear service information
- Personalized customer experience
- Hair and beauty options
- Consultation before selecting suitable services
- Convenient Bengaluru location

Do not make claims that cannot be verified.

---

## Local Context

Naturally incorporate the location:

Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

The content may reference Bengaluru customers and the local
business context where appropriate.

Do not overuse location keywords.

---

Naturally consider relevant search terms such as:

- salon in Bangalore
- hair salon in Bangalore
- hair colour Bangalore
- hair styling Bangalore
- beauty services Bangalore
- hair treatments Bangalore
- curls and texture salon

Use keywords naturally.

Do not keyword-stuff or sacrifice readability for SEO.

---

## CTA Requirements

Create clear but natural calls-to-action.

Examples of CTA intent include:

- Booking an appointment
- Exploring services
- Exploring hair services
- Exploring hair colour
- Exploring beauty services
- Exploring hair treatments
- Booking a consultation

Do not create fake urgency such as "Book now before it's too late"
unless the business has provided a real limited-time offer.
