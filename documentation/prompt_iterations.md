# Prompt Iteration and Evaluation

## Purpose

The prompt system was refined through multiple stages to improve
specificity, relevance, accuracy, and website usability.

---

## Iteration 1 – Basic Prompt

### Approach

The initial concept asked the AI to generate website copy for
a local salon.

### Problem

A broad prompt can produce generic marketing language and may
introduce unsupported business information.

---

## Iteration 2 – Add Business Context

The prompt was improved by providing:

- Business name
- Business type
- Location
- Target audience
- Services

### Improvement

The output became more specific to the selected business and
its customers.

---

## Iteration 3 – Add Structured Output

The prompt was then divided into specific website sections:

- Hero section
- Introduction
- Benefits
- Services
- CTA

### Improvement

The generated content became easier to review and directly
usable as website sections.

---

## Iteration 4 – Add Tone Controls

The prompt specified:

- Friendly
- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Persuasive

### Improvement

The tone became more consistent and appropriate for a hair and
beauty business.

---

## Iteration 5 – Add Accuracy Constraints

The final prompt instructs the AI not to invent:

- Prices
- Discounts
- Certifications
- Guarantees
- Staff qualifications
- Unsupported services
- Unsupported treatment results

### Improvement

This reduces the risk of hallucinated business information and
makes the output more appropriate for real-world client work.

---

## Final Prompt Framework

The final system uses:

Role
→ Business Context
→ Target Audience
→ Services
→ Objective
→ Content Requirements
→ Tone
→ Local Context
→ Accuracy Constraints
→ Output Format

---

## Evaluation Criteria

The final outputs were evaluated for:

- Business relevance
- Clarity
- Specificity
- CTA strength
- Tone consistency
- Website usability
- Accuracy
- Reusability

---

## Result

The final prompt system provides a reusable framework for
creating website copy for real local businesses while reducing
generic or unsupported AI-generated content.
